## #12(a) — Fable's re-offer rule

- **#12(a) Verdict: ACCEPT WITH BOUND + `answers` LINKAGE — yes, naive re-offer CAN double-deliver.** Interleaving: (1) Mick posts `X` (`"Status?"`); router commits `X` and delivers to `M1` (term 1). (2) `M1` answers `X` directly via `bus_post(to="human", ...)` — today neither `bus_post` nor `channel_post` links to `X`, so no assignment or answer record exists for `X` (or `M1` had two open posts `X1`/`X2` and answered only `X1`, or died right after Discord mirror). (3) `M1` dies; router promotes `M2` (term 2) and re-offers `X`. (4) `M2` answers `X` a second time or assigns a worker to redo already-answered work.
- **Fix:** Add optional `answers: msgId` on the master's `to="human"` post (atomically recording `status:'answered'` in the #1 assignment table alongside the Discord mirror), and bound re-offer to recent unresolved posts (e.g., last 5 min) tagged `reoffered: true` so `M2` checks channel history (`fetch_messages`) before acting instead of blindly re-answering.
