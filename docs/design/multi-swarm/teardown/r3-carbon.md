## Findings

### 1. [BLOCKER] Client-side `<swarm>/<dir>` naming & normalisation (`spec-v3.md:41,126-133,224`) breaks Rollout Step 1 and renames tmux `"0"` sessions
- **Problem:** `defaultSessions` (`["agents", "0"]`) and config presence live on `vps2` (`spec-v3.md:18,23,34`), unreachable from client hosts. Yet §5 (`spec-v3.md:130`) builds `<agent>@<host>:<swarm>/<dir>` *before* `register` (`src/plugin.ts:160,453`), and `WsRegistered` (`src/types.ts:258`) omits normalised `swarm` and `defaultSessions`. Even without config in Rollout Step 1 (`spec-v3.md:224`), PR2 makes every plugin in tmux `"0"` (`claude@mac:swarm-master` in `~/src/swarm-master` without `DISCORD_SESSION_NAME`, `agy@nas642:p1`, `claude@mac:bbq`) treat `"0"` as non-default (`claude@mac:0/swarm-master`), breaking `default` back-compat (`spec-v3.md:13`), `crossSwarm`, and candidacy. Without `WsRegistered.{swarm, defaultSessions}`, `plugin.ts` also cannot normalise `DISCORD_WAKE_PANE` (`spec-v3.md:132`) or detect normalised-swarm changes on the 10 s timer (`spec-v3.md:133`). Finally, `findTmuxPane` (`src/plugin.ts:228`) returns `#{pane_id}` (`%N`), not `#{window_id}` (`@N`) (`spec-v3.md:126`).
- **Fix (spec text):** *"Plugin sends raw `swarm` (`session_group || session_name`, else `SWARM_ID`) plus `generated?: true` with base `<agent>@<host>:<dir>` on `register`. Router normalises `swarm`, rewrites generated non-default names to `<agent>@<host>:<swarm>/<dir>` before collision/lease checks, and returns `{swarm, defaultSessions}` on `WsRegistered` (`types.ts:258`) for `assignedAlias`, `DISCORD_WAKE_PANE`, and the 10 s re-check. Read `#{pane_pid} #{pane_id} #{window_id}` in `findTmuxPane` (`plugin.ts:228`), refreshing `pane_id → window_id` each tick."*

Collide (`-N`) when `knownSwarm(candidate)` is defined and `!== callerSwarm` on both `register` and `rename` (`ws.ts:843,967,1002`), never overwriting `agentSwarms` across swarms. Refuse cross-swarm `registerAgent` before `inboxes.bind` (`ws.ts:528`) and non-`default` aliases in `registerNode` (`ws.ts:680`). Key `subs.reclaim` by `${host ?? ''}:${instanceId}` (when `instanceId` set) and `explicitAlias` (`plugin.ts:308`) by `${swarm}:${instanceId}`.
