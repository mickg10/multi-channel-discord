## Findings

### 1. [BLOCKER] `protectedRenameAlias` at `register` (`spec-v2.md:24-26,132`) is absent in `ws.ts` and locks out real cross-swarm & candidate seats
- **Problem:** `protectedRenameAlias` (`src/ws.ts:443-452`) runs only on `rename` (`src/ws.ts:885,1002`) and PR #26 `observeDirectChannel` (`src/ws.ts:184`), never `register` (`src/ws.ts:818-878`). Plugins share `DISCORD_ROUTER_SECRET` (`spec-v2.md:14`), so rejecting `protectedRenameAlias` at `register` (`spec-v2.md:24,132`) blocks the real `claude@mac:ops-tmux-fixer` and master candidates from registering, breaks Claude `/name` watch (`src/plugin.ts:534` sends `rename`), and suppresses PR #26 direct channels for `ops-tmux-fixer` (`src/ws.ts:184` on PR #26).
- **Fix (spec text):** *"Check `protectedRenameAlias` (`crossSwarm`+`candidates`) on `rename` (`ws.ts:885,1002`) only. At `register` (`ws.ts:830`), reject `crossSwarm` aliases when `swarm !== 'default'` and `candidates` aliases when `swarm !== candidateSwarm`. Exclude `candidates` (not `crossSwarm`) from `observeDirectChannel`; launch both via `DISCORD_SESSION_NAME`, not `/name`."*

### 2. [BLOCKER] Closing old sockets on duplicate `instanceId` (`spec-v2.md:32`) breaks Claude multi-MCP dedup; bare-PID `instanceId` collides across hosts
- **Problem:** Claude spawns several MCP processes per session sharing `ppid` (`src/plugin.ts:128-132,148-151`); `registerSession` (`src/sessions.ts:39-45`) merges them into `slot.extraSends`. Closing old sockets on duplicate `instanceId` (`spec-v2.md:32`) triggers `scheduleReconnect()` (`src/plugin.ts:502`) in an infinite eviction loop, while bare-PID `instanceId` collides in `sessions.ts:41` and PR #27 `NameLeases.hold` (`src/name-leases.ts:102`) across `mac`, `nas642`, and `nas64`.
- **Fix (spec text):** *"Key `instanceId` in `SessionManager` (`sessions.ts:41`) and `NameLeases` (`name-leases.ts:102`) by `${host}:${instanceId}`. Merge into `extraSends` when `(host, instanceId)` and `slot.swarm === swarm` match; close old sockets and deregister only when `slot.swarm !== swarm` (`move-window`) or on config reload."*
